# Long-term systems and expansion ideas

## Class advancement

Each base class has one promotion path. Level 25, base M20 and the Promotion Trial gate advancement for each of the two learned classes. [Promotion rules](C5_LONG_TERM.md)

## Seasons

The first two seasons last six weeks and open tiers weekly. Each has a boss ladder, rule, Relic and title track. Server-validated leaderboards retain standings per season. Only the Relic resets. [Season rules](C5_LONG_TERM.md)

## Dungeons

Asynchronous parties of three characters. Every character remains solo capable; no mandatory Tank/Healer/DPS slots. Builds contribute single-target damage, AoE, Crit, Burn, Poison, Bleed, armor pressure and buffs/debuffs. Encounters should reward complementary capabilities without requiring one class.

Briar Vault borrows opted-in friends and guildmates through versioned build snapshots. Game-core simulates each of the three characters. The authority commits eligible rewards with the attempt receipt. [Dungeon rules](../technical/ONLINE_AUTHORITY.md#friends-dungeons-and-raids)

## Guilds

Guild membership, dungeon-earned marks and the Expedition Table support the first raid. Achievements, expeditions, cosmetics and merchants remain expansion ideas.

Avoid enormous permanent passive multipliers (+100% damage/XP), account-age snowball and a reward structure that makes newer guilds irrelevant. Prefer unlocking shared activities/options over multiplying all personal progression.

Guild Hall, Blacksmith, Alchemist, Raid Hall and Trophy Hall are possible additional facilities.

## Raids

Cinder Breach combines boss damage and defeated adds across three phases. Additional encounters could reward statuses, armor pressure or other objectives. [Raid rules](../technical/ONLINE_AUTHORITY.md#friends-dungeons-and-raids)

## Live operations

The authority loads versioned season rotations and serves the event calendar, reward tracks and leaderboards. Invasions and rare encounters remain expansion ideas. [Scheduled rotations](../technical/ONLINE_AUTHORITY.md#scheduled-rotations)
