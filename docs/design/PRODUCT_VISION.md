# Wyrdholt: Idle — product vision

## DECIDED / NOT IMPLEMENTED — full product ambition

A portrait mobile asynchronous fantasy RPG for people without time for an active MMORPG. Execution is idle; strategy is not. One persistent character grows through class mechanics, named equipment and content choices. No rebirth and no permanent-character seasonal reset. Native iOS/Android is the product; browser access is a remote playtest surface.

The desired loop is Push → fail → Farm → notice loot → understand and change a build → retry. Automation belongs within selected content; entering new content is a player decision. No mandatory MMO trinity; every class must remain solo capable.

## IMPLEMENTED — current slice

C1 includes Monk/Mage, three Adventure zones, Mastery, gear comparison, deterministic offline catch-up and persistence. A/B are complete. The C1 retune awaits an Android playtest on the REDMAGIC 11 Pro. The current C2-based integration adds Warrior/Ranger and Mage Ward; four-class human validation remains open. Broader MMORPG ambitions are not shipped functionality. See [systems](GAMEPLAY_SYSTEMS.md).

## DECIDED / NOT IMPLEMENTED — boundaries

Named items and mechanic synergy should support choices, not one universal Power Score or Equip Best. Future social content is asynchronous. Monetization must not confer paid competitive dominance; revenue concepts in PRODUCT.md remain future options, not current systems. Visual direction is restrained dark forest fantasy, warm amber, moss and ivory, compatible with handcrafted pixel art.

## OPEN — validation

Actual pacing, failure/retry satisfaction and alternate-class learning must be validated by people. The precise launch scope, commercial offers and final tuning are not decided by UI mockups. Read ROADMAP for milestone ownership; do not infer permission to implement from this vision.
