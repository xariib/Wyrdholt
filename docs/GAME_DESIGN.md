# Game design — implemented behavior (Phase B + C1)

Values marked *provisional* live in `packages/game-content` and are placeholders, not final balance. C1 pacing and class numbers come from simulation against approved targets: see [design/C1_ADVENTURE.md](design/C1_ADVENTURE.md), reports/C1_PACING.md and reports/C1_CLASS_BALANCE.md.

## Character

- **One character.** Its name is 2–16 characters.
- **Character Level** is shared by both classes, with a cap of 30. XP per level is an explicit table (`LEVEL_XP_STEPS`), fitted so a player who checks in about three times a day reaches:
  - level 5–6 in the first 30-minute session
  - level 12 around day 2
  - level 19 around day 7
  - level 30 after about 12 weeks
- **Two learned classes**, the Active Class and the Alternate Class, both chosen at creation from the implemented base classes (Monk, Mage; on `c2-warrior-ranger` also Warrior and Ranger).
- **Switching classes:** the Alternate can be made Active at any time. During a fight the switch is queued (see build snapshot rule).
- **Starter kit:** six Common starter items, equipped as item instances.

## Classes and Class Mastery

- **Monk and Mage** kits follow MASTERY_AUDIT, with unlocks at M1 / M5 / M10 / M15 / M20. C1 changed some numbers; see [technical/ITEMS_CLASSES_CONTENT.md](technical/ITEMS_CLASSES_CONTENT.md).
- **Warrior and Ranger** (C2, branch `c2-warrior-ranger`): Warrior is slow and heavy (Bleed, Retaliation, Execute, no healing); Ranger is Poison, crits, aimed extra arrows and armor pierce. Both are calibrated to the Monk/Mage walls (reports/C2_FOUR_CLASS.md).
- **Immediately viable, gradually specialized.** An M1 class clears the opening fight. At M20, the mechanics add more damage than the flat foundation (tested).
- **Mastery XP is separate from character XP** and goes to the class that fought:
  - ordinary stage: `round(2 + 0.4 × stage level)`, unless the zone overrides it
  - Elite: 3×; Zone Boss: 3×
  - completion cache: +10 / +20 / +35 (Greenfields / Darkwood / Ashen)
  - XP per rank is an explicit table (`MASTERY_XP_STEPS`): M5 on day 1, M10 around day 3, M15 around day 7–8, M20 after day 21
- **Class targets (C1, extended to four classes in C2):** in every zone, all implemented classes hit the wall within 1 level of each other, and their normal fights differ in length by at most 25%.
  - A sanity check also guards against extreme splits at the same progression state: survivability, Boss fight length, agreement one level either side, and first-clear days.
  - Everything passes (`tools/sim/class-report.ts`, `tools/sim/pacing.ts`).
- **Mage survivability:** on the C1 branches the Mage shows more HP and armor than the Monk (the Monk survives by healing). On `c2-warrior-ranger` the Mage is frailer than the Monk (100 + 10/level HP, +1 armor/level) and survives with **Arcane Ward** (M1): every fourth spell adds 7% attack to a ward that absorbs damage before HP, up to 20% max HP, and fades after each fight. The Mage's walls are unchanged.

## Zones

Three playable zones, entered in order. Each has ten stages: stages 1–4 and 6–9 are normal, stage 5 is the **Elite**, stage 10 is the **Zone Boss**. One stage is one encounter with 1–3 simultaneous enemies.

| Stage | Greenfields (levels 1–6) | Darkwood (levels 7–14) | Ashen Mountains (levels 14–20) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Meadow Slime | 2 × Gloom Spider | Ash Hound + Cinder Imp |
| 2 | 2 × Meadow Slime | Barkhide Brute | Basalt Golem |
| 3 | Bristle Boar | Hollow Wisp + Gloom Spider | 2 × Cinder Imp + Ash Hound |
| 4 | Field Wolf + Meadow Slime | 2 × Gloom Spider + Hollow Wisp | 2 × Ash Hound |
| 5 **Elite** | Thornback Boar | Thornwarden | Ember Warlord |
| 6 | Bristle Boar + Field Wolf | Barkhide Brute + Hollow Wisp | Basalt Golem + Cinder Imp |
| 7 | 2 × Field Wolf + Meadow Slime | 2 × Hollow Wisp + Gloom Spider | Ash Hound + 2 × Cinder Imp |
| 8 | 2 × Bristle Boar | 2 × Barkhide Brute | Basalt Golem + Ash Hound |
| 9 | Field Wolf + Bristle Boar + Field Wolf | Gloom Spider + Barkhide Brute + Hollow Wisp | Cinder Imp + Basalt Golem + Ash Hound |
| 10 **Boss** | Elder Root + Sproutling | **Hollow Matron** + 2 × Matron's Brood | **Caldera Drake** |

- **Names:** the bosses come from the Godot reports. The other Darkwood and Ashen enemies, and all new sprites and backgrounds, are provisional placeholders.
- **Unlocks:** Greenfields → Darkwood → Ashen Mountains. A newly unlocked zone is **never entered automatically**; the player taps **Begin** or **Push** in the zone list.
- **Budgets:** every stage has one HP budget and one damage-per-second budget. Group members **share** them by archetype weight, so enemy count changes the problem (more attacks, more value for Wildfire and chain lightning) rather than multiplying difficulty.
  - The budgets are generated by `tools/sim/tune-budgets.ts` against the build a player has at each wall (Greenfields L5 M2 with starter gear; Darkwood L12 M9 with the Greenfields Rare set; Ashen L19 M14 with the Darkwood Epic set).
  - At that build, fights last about 10 s (normal), 20 s (Elite) and 36 s (Boss).
- **Walls are sharp.** Push outcomes barely vary: about one level (or a mastery rank or two, or a gear tier) decides a Boss.

| Rewards | Greenfields | Darkwood | Ashen Mountains |
|---|---|---|---|
| Stage XP | 4 + 2 × level | 8 + 3 × level | 12 + 4 × level |
| Stage gold | 2 + 2 × level | 4 + 3 × level | 6 + 4 × level |
| Elite / Boss multiplier (XP, gold) | ×3 / ×6 | ×3 / ×6 | ×3 / ×6 |
| Completion cache (XP / gold / mastery) | 40 / 60 / 10 | 150 / 200 / 20 | 400 / 500 / 35 |
| First-clear gold (once) | 100 | 300 | 600 |

**Loot.** Stage drops use the zone's farm table (the Boss stage uses its boss table).

| Zone | Stage drop | Boss drop | Rarities (farm table) | Max |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Greenfields | 1.5% | 4% | C/U/R 48/34/18 | Rare |
| Darkwood | 1.5% | 4% | C/U/R/E 32/35/24/9 | Epic |
| Ashen Mountains | 1.5% | 4% | C/U/R/E/L 20/31/33/13/3 | Legendary |

- **Short fights drop less.** A stage drop rolls at full chance for victories of at least 10 s, and proportionally less for shorter ones. Farming far below your power therefore gives about the same drops per hour, not a flood.
- **Completion cache:** the first clear always rolls the zone's cache table (100% drop, weighted toward Rare+). Repeat clears roll it 4% of the time.
- **Item levels:** Rare items need encounter level 5, Epics level 9, Legendaries level 13.
- **Result:** about 30–60 items per 8-hour night (simulation median 46–50).

## Runs, HP and death

- A new run starts at full HP. **HP carries between stages**, plus **18% of max HP** recovery after each non-final victory. There is never a full heal, and level-ups do not heal.
- **Rest times:** 1.2 s between stages, 2 s after a clear, 6 s after an offline defeat before the fallback run.
- **Death (or a 120 s timeout) ends the run.** Earned XP, gold, items and mastery stay; the defeated fight grants nothing.
  - **Online:** the activity stops. The result screen shows the stage reached, the enemy HP left, the stage's enemy level next to the player's level and mastery, and offers **Retry Push**, **Change build** and **Farm** the strongest cleared zone (or the Greenfields trail when nothing is cleared).
  - **Offline:** see below.
- **Checkpoint:** the stage start (stage, HP, seed) plus progress into the current fight or rest. Leaving and returning replays the same fight from the same seed and HP.

## Push and Farm

- There is no Pause, no manual Save Run and no manual next stage.
- **Push** attempts the selected **uncleared** zone. Stages continue automatically. The final victory settles the cache, marks the clear, unlocks the next zone, then **farms the zone just cleared**.
- **Farm:**
  - on a cleared zone, it repeats that zone, including the boss and cache
  - before the first clear, it repeats that zone's **trail** (stage 1) with no unlocks or cache
- **Switching activity** ends the current attempt cleanly. A Push keeps its checkpoint (same seed, stage and HP) and resumes it on the next Push.

## Build snapshot rule

- **During an active fight,** class and gear changes are **queued** and apply only after that fight ends. The fight resolves with the build it started with, and its rewards and mastery go to the class that fought.
- **Outside a fight,** changes apply immediately (from the next fight).
- **Queued changes** can be cancelled. Queued instances are reserved.
- **When a new build applies between stages,** carried HP is clamped to the new maximum and never refilled.

## Offline progression

- **Scope:** uses the same pipeline as online play and is capped at 8 hours.
- **Push offline:**
  - **Failure:** the failure point is recorded, risky Push stops, and the rest of the time is spent farming safe content (the strongest cleared zone, otherwise the trail).
  - **Success:** the next zone unlocks, is **not** entered, and the rest of the time is spent farming the zone just cleared.
- **Failed Farm:** steps down to weaker content; if even the trail fails, progress stops.
- **Settlement** is idempotent, and a clock that moved backwards settles nothing.
- **Return summary:**
  - Push result
  - time per activity
  - XP, gold and level change
  - at most three distinct notable finds (Rare+ first, with Compare buttons)
  - "+N other items"
  - a "Review new gear" button

## Gear

- **Loadout hub:** all six slots are visible, each with its rarity and a NEW count.
- **Slot sheet:** tapping a slot opens its alternatives, grouped by catalog item (with a copy count) and sorted equipped → new → rarity.
- **Comparison:** tapping an item shows current vs candidate with raw stats and ▲/▼ deltas, plus both special effects. **Equip** returns to the loadout.
- **Build summary:**
  - Health, Attack, Armor, **Attack rate in attacks per second**, Crit, Mastery
  - active effects (class techniques and item effects)
  - descriptive tags
- **Not included:** Equip Best and a Power Score.
- **Toasts:** only Rare+ finds get one. Everything else only updates the Gear badge.

## Adventure screen (C1)

- **Current zone card:** name, stage dots (Elite and Boss marked), activity.
- **Combat view:** each zone has its own procedural background.
- **This run:** carried HP, run totals, best stage.
- **Push / Farm** for the current zone.
- **Begin <next zone>** card when a newly unlocked zone is waiting.
- **Zone list:** every zone with its enemy levels and status (locked with its requirement, best stage, or cleared ×N), and a Push or Farm button.
- **Developer tools** can unlock all zones for visual checks. This spoils a pacing playtest.

## Not in this build

- Warrior/Ranger kits (C1 branches only; they are on `c2-warrior-ranger`)
- named effects for the new Epic and Legendary items (stats only)
- Forge, the Solo Boss ladder, Artifacts
- promotions, seasons, Ancient items, random affixes
- guilds, dungeons, raids, purchases, backend
